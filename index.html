Privacy Policy — DIT Diploma: Complete Course

Last updated: 25 November 2026

App: DIT Diploma: Complete Course (com.LearnDITCourse)

Developer: Tariq Ahmed — tariqktk2015@gmail.com

DIT Diploma: Complete Course is an offline-first learning app that teaches the DIT Diploma course step by step in Urdu and English. This policy explains what information the app and its service providers handle.

1. Information we collect

We collect no personal information from you. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other directly identifying personal information, and the app has no account or sign-in.

The only information handled to operate the app is:

  • Purchase information: records of in-app purchases (which premium product was bought, e.g. Semester 2 access) so the app can unlock the content you paid for. Payment details are never collected by us — all payments are processed by Google Play.
  • Course images: lesson images are downloaded from our image host (Netlify) when you first open a course. Like most web servers, the host may keep standard server logs (such as your IP address) for operating the service.

2. How we use the information

  • In-app purchases — to unlock Semester 2 for users who buy it (via Google Play Billing). Purchases are verified with Google Play each time the app starts.
  • App functionality — downloading course images on first launch and daily learning reminders (09:00, 15:00 and 20:00).

3. Sharing with third parties

We share information only with the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Google Play Billing — purchase processing, handled entirely by Google Play.
  • Netlify — hosting of course images, processed under Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Google Play — in-app review prompts and links to other apps on Google Play.

We do not sell your information. This app shows no ads and uses no advertising SDK.

4. Information stored only on your device

The following never leave your phone and are stored only in the app's local storage:

  • Lesson progress, quiz results, and final exam progress, answers and scores
  • Your language choice (English / Urdu) and daily reminder preference
  • Cached course images, so lessons open quickly after the first download

5. Your choices

  • Notifications: daily reminders can be turned off anytime in the app's Settings screen or in your device notification settings.
  • Restore purchases: if you bought Semester 2, you can restore it from the purchase screen — purchases are tied to your Google account.
  • Clear cached images: you can clear the image cache anytime from the app's Settings screen.

6. Permissions

The app may request notification permission (for daily learning reminders). Internet access is used to download course images and to process in-app purchases; the lessons themselves work offline once images are downloaded. No other special device permissions are required.

7. Children

The app is a general educational tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.
